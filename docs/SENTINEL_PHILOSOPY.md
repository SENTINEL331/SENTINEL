# Sentinel Philosophy

Sentinel exists to generate profitable trading opportunities through disciplined research, rigorous validation, controlled risk and continuous learning.

## Principles

- Profit with controlled risk.
- Evidence before opinion.
- Every recommendation must be explainable.
- Nothing is forgotten.
- AI proposes. Sentinel proves.
- No trade is a valid outcome.
- Research never stops.
## Design Principles

- Build reliable foundations before AI.
- AI performs research, not deterministic calculations.
- Every module has a single responsibility.
- Every feature must justify its existence through evidence.
- Memory is a first-class component of Sentinel.
- Every decision should be explainable.
