# Long-Term Vision

Sentinel is not an automated trading bot.

Sentinel is an AI Trading Research Platform whose purpose is to discover,
test, validate and monitor trading hypotheses through a structured
research pipeline.

Sentinel is responsible for collecting evidence.

Artificial Intelligence is responsible for interpreting that evidence.

Trading decisions are made only after a hypothesis has successfully
progressed through every stage of validation.

## Research Pipeline

Stage 1
--------
Market Data Collection

- Download market data
- Validate historical data
- Build feature datasets

Stage 2
--------
Hypothesis Generation

- AI generates trading hypotheses
- No trades are taken

Stage 3
--------
Hypothesis Testing

- Historical testing
- Statistical validation
- Performance measurements

Stage 4
--------
Research Validation

Only hypotheses meeting minimum research standards continue.

Stage 5
--------
Paper Trading

Successful hypotheses are promoted to a simulated environment.

Performance is continuously monitored.

Stage 6
--------
Live Trading Approval

Only consistently successful strategies become eligible for live trading.

Promotion requires predefined performance criteria.

Stage 7
--------
Portfolio Management

Approved strategies continue to be monitored.

Poorly performing strategies may be automatically demoted or retired.
