# Sentinel Research Framework

## Mission

Sentinel is an autonomous quantitative research laboratory.

Its purpose is to provide objective market evidence and reliable execution capabilities to an AI researcher.

Sentinel does not generate opinions, investment advice, or trading decisions.

The AI performs research.

Sentinel performs execution.

---

# Sentinel Laws

## Law 1

Sentinel measures.

AI interprets.

---

## Law 2

Sentinel executes.

AI decides.

---

## Law 3

Sentinel stores facts.

AI stores research.

---

## Law 4

Every hypothesis must originate from an observation.

---

## Law 5

Every promotion must be earned through new evidence.

---

## Law 6

Research capacity is limited.

Each symbol may have a maximum number of active hypotheses.

A new hypothesis may only be created if capacity exists or an existing hypothesis is retired.

---

## Law 7

Research never resets.

The AI continues previous research rather than starting again each day.

---

## Law 8

The burden of proof increases with promotion.

Every stage requires stronger evidence than the last.

---

# Responsibilities

## Sentinel

Sentinel is responsible for:

- Collecting market data
- Calculating measurements
- Executing experiments
- Running backtests
- Managing paper trading
- Managing live execution
- Recording objective results

Sentinel never generates opinions.

---

## AI Researcher

The AI is responsible for:

- Reviewing observations
- Creating hypotheses
- Prioritising research
- Requesting experiments
- Reviewing results
- Promoting or retiring strategies

The AI never directly executes trades.

# Promotion Rules

A hypothesis must satisfy every stage before promotion.

Passing one stage does not guarantee promotion.

Promotion requires sufficient evidence.

Failure at any stage returns the hypothesis to research or retirement.

---

# Research Capacity

Research capacity is intentionally limited.

Initial configuration:

Maximum Active Hypotheses per Symbol = 6

The AI must continuously prioritise which hypotheses deserve its attention.

---

# Core Philosophy

Evidence before execution.

Nothing is promoted because it appears promising.

Everything earns promotion through objective evidence.
